# Blinkit Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Business Analytics case study focused on analyzing Blinkit's business performance using SQL and Power BI.

The objective of this project is to identify the root causes behind declining customer satisfaction, inconsistent delivery performance, inventory optimization challenges, customer purchasing behavior, and marketing effectiveness. Using SQL for data analysis and Power BI for interactive visualization, the project provides actionable business insights and strategic recommendations to improve overall business performance.

---

## Business Problem

Blinkit experienced declining customer satisfaction, inconsistent delivery performance, inventory optimization challenges, and varying marketing effectiveness.

The objective of this project is to analyze operational, customer, product, and marketing data to identify business issues and recommend data-driven solutions that improve customer experience and operational efficiency.

---

## Business Objectives

- Improve delivery performance
- Increase customer satisfaction
- Optimize inventory management
- Increase repeat customers
- Improve marketing performance
- Support business decision-making through data analytics

---

## Business Questions

### 1. How can Blinkit improve customer satisfaction while reducing delivery-related complaints?

### 2. How can Blinkit increase profit by optimizing product inventory?

### 3. Which customer segments generate the highest lifetime value and how should Blinkit target them?

### 4. Which marketing campaigns deliver the highest return on investment (ROI)?

### 5. Why are some customers dissatisfied and what factors influence negative feedback?

## Key Business Insights

- Total revenue exceeded ₹11 million across 5,000 customer orders.
- Delivery success rate is 69.40%, indicating opportunities for improving delivery efficiency.
- Dairy & Breakfast is the highest revenue-generating product category.
- Vitamins generated the highest product revenue.
- Regular customers contribute the highest overall revenue.
- New customers have the highest average order value.
- Product Quality generated the highest number of negative customer complaints.
- Referral Program generated the highest marketing revenue.
- Email marketing generated the highest overall channel revenue.
- Average marketing ROAS of 2.74 indicates effective marketing performance.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

### Customer Experience

- Improve delivery route optimization.
- Strengthen product quality checks.
- Improve customer service response time.
- Reduce delivery-related complaints.

### Inventory Management

- Maintain higher stock levels for high-demand products.
- Reduce inventory for slow-moving products.
- Prioritize high-margin products during promotions.

### Customer Retention

- Reward loyal customers through loyalty programs.
- Target high-value customers using personalized offers.
- Encourage new customers to become repeat buyers.

### Marketing

- Increase investment in Referral Program campaigns.
- Expand Email marketing initiatives.
- Optimize low-performing campaigns using A/B testing.
- Improve campaign conversion rates.
